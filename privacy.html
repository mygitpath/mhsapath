1. Overview

myHSAPath.com ("myHSAPath", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of our visitors and subscribers. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and what rights you have over your data.

This policy applies to all information collected through our website at myhsapath.com, including our blog, comparison tools, calculators, eligibility quiz, and newsletter signup forms.

By using myHSAPath, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Site.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Newsletter delivery: Sending our biweekly HSA newsletter and related educational content to subscribers who opted in
  • Site improvement: Understanding which pages and content are most useful so we can improve the site
  • Affiliate tracking: Confirming when a referred account opening occurred so we can receive the correct commission
  • Legal compliance: Maintaining records required by applicable tax and business laws
  • Customer support: Responding to emails and questions sent to us directly

We do not use your information for targeted advertising, sell it to data brokers, share it with insurance companies or financial institutions without your consent, or use it for any automated decision-making that affects you.

4. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Good news: myHSAPath uses Plausible Analytics, which is a privacy-friendly analytics tool that does not use cookies and does not track visitors across websites or devices. We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any other cookie-based tracking.

What we use

  • Plausible Analytics: Cookie-free, GDPR-compliant analytics. Plausible uses a privacy-preserving method to count unique visitors without storing any personal data or using cookies. No consent banner is required.
  • Beehiiv newsletter: When you subscribe to our newsletter, Beehiiv may set a cookie to manage your subscription preferences and track email opens and clicks. You can opt out by unsubscribing.
  • Affiliate network cookies: When you click an affiliate link to a custodian (Fidelity, Lively, etc.), the affiliate network (Impact.com or CJ Affiliate) may set a tracking cookie on the custodian's site to attribute the referral. This is governed by that network's and custodian's own privacy policies.

What we do not use

  • Google Analytics or any Google tracking products
  • Facebook Pixel or Meta tracking
  • Advertising cookies or retargeting pixels
  • Session recording tools (Hotjar, FullStory, etc.)
  • Any first-party cookies for advertising purposes

6. Affiliate Link Data

myHSAPath participates in affiliate marketing programs. When you click a link to an HSA custodian on our site, the following may occur:

  • The affiliate network (Impact.com or CJ Affiliate) records the click and sets a tracking cookie on the destination site
  • If you open an account within the cookie window (typically 30–90 days), the network attributes the referral to myHSAPath and we receive a commission
  • The custodian (Fidelity, Lively, HealthEquity, etc.) receives your account application data directly — we never see your account details, SSN, financial information, or any data you provide during account opening

We never see your HSA account details. When you open an account through our links, all account data goes directly to the custodian. We only receive a confirmation that a qualifying referral occurred — not your name, account number, or any financial details.

9. Children's Privacy

myHSAPath is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. HSA accounts require adult legal capacity to open, and our content is designed for adults managing their own health and financial decisions.

If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately at privacy@myhsapath.com and we will delete it promptly.

10. International Users & Cross-Border Data Transfers

myHSAPath is operated from Canada and primarily serves users in the United States. By using our site, your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in Canada, the United States, or other countries where our service providers operate.

For European Union / EEA users (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Union or European Economic Area, you have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Our legal basis for processing your personal data is:

  • Consent — for newsletter subscriptions and email communications
  • Legitimate interests — for privacy-friendly analytics to improve our site
  • Contract performance — where we need to process data to fulfill a service you requested

You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority if you believe your rights have been violated.

For Canadian users (PIPEDA)

As a Canadian-operated site, we comply with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). We collect only the minimum personal information necessary and obtain meaningful consent before collecting email addresses. You have the right to access and correct your personal information held by us.

11. California Residents (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) gives you specific rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to know: You can request information about the personal data we collect, use, disclose, and sell (we do not sell personal data)
  • Right to delete: You can request deletion of personal data we have collected from you
  • Right to opt-out of sale: We do not sell personal information — no opt-out is needed
  • Right to non-discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising your CCPA rights

To exercise your California privacy rights, email privacy@myhsapath.com with "California Privacy Request" in the subject line.

12. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

  • HTTPS encryption on all pages of the site (SSL/TLS)
  • Secure, reputable third-party processors for email (Beehiiv) and hosting (Netlify)
  • Minimal data collection — we only collect what we need
  • No storage of financial data, account numbers, or sensitive personal information

However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

In the event of a data breach that affects your personal information, we will notify affected users as required by applicable law.
