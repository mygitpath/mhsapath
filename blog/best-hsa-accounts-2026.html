2026 update: All ACA Bronze and Catastrophic plans are now HSA-eligible following the One Big Beautiful Bill Act. This is the biggest expansion of HSA access in years — millions more Americans now qualify. If you've been on a Bronze plan and didn't think you could open an HSA, check again.

Health Savings Accounts are the most powerful tax tool available to self-employed Americans. The triple tax advantage — pre-tax contributions, tax-free growth, tax-free withdrawals — is unmatched by any other savings vehicle. But picking the wrong custodian can cost you hundreds of dollars a year in unnecessary fees.

We compared every major HSA custodian on the four things that actually matter: monthly fees, investment options, user experience, and institutional trust. Here's what we found.

Quick summary — our top picks

  • Best overall: Fidelity HSA — zero fees, invest from $1, 1,000+ fund options
  • Best for freelancers: Lively HSA — best UX, fastest account opening, zero fees
  • Best for employer plans: HealthEquity — largest employer network, fee waived at $2,500
  • Best for large balances: HSA Bank — established institution, Devenir investment platform

1. Fidelity HSA — Best Overall

Fidelity's HSA is the rare product that wins on every dimension. Zero monthly fees. Zero investment fees. The ability to invest from your very first dollar with access to over 1,000 mutual funds — including Fidelity's own zero-expense-ratio index funds. For self-employed Americans who want both spending flexibility today and long-term investment growth, nothing else comes close.

The key differentiator: Most HSA custodians require a $1,000 cash balance before you can invest. Fidelity lets you invest from $1. On a $4,300 annual contribution, that difference in compound growth adds up to thousands of dollars over a decade.

The main downside is that Fidelity's HSA interface is part of their broader brokerage platform, not a dedicated HSA experience. If you want a cleaner, HSA-specific dashboard, Lively does that better. But for pure financial performance, Fidelity is unmatched.

2. Lively HSA — Best for Freelancers

Lively was purpose-built for the self-employed experience — and the difference shows. Account opening takes under 5 minutes. The dashboard is the cleanest of any HSA we tested, built specifically for HSA management rather than bolted onto a broader brokerage platform. Zero fees make it instantly competitive with Fidelity on cost.

Where Lively falls slightly behind is investment depth. Their investment account runs through TD Ameritrade, which requires a separate login — a minor friction point that Fidelity avoids. Interest rates on cash balances are also below average.

3. HealthEquity — Best for Employer Plans

HealthEquity is the largest HSA custodian in the US by assets and its strength is employer integrations. If your client or a company you contract with offers an employer HSA, there's a good chance it runs through HealthEquity. The $2.50/month fee is waived once you hit a $2,500 balance, which most HSA holders reach within a year of contributions.

For purely individual self-employed accounts starting from zero, Fidelity and Lively are more attractive. But HealthEquity earns its third-place ranking for the depth of its compliance tools and employer network.

4. HSA Bank — Best for Large Balances

HSA Bank has been a dedicated HSA provider since 2004 — one of the longest track records in the industry. The $3/month fee and $5,000 minimum to waive it make it a tough sell for accounts just getting started. For people with substantial existing balances who want an established institution with deep HSA expertise, it's a credible option.

How to choose the right HSA custodian

The right answer depends on where you are financially:

  • Just starting out, low balance: Fidelity or Lively. Both are free, both let you get started immediately without a minimum balance requirement.
  • Want the cleanest mobile experience: Lively. Their app is the best-designed HSA interface we tested.
  • Want the most investment options: Fidelity. 1,000+ funds with zero-expense-ratio options beats every competitor.
  • Have an employer HDHP: Check whether your employer already has a relationship with HealthEquity — employer contributions may make it the default best choice.
  • Already have a large HSA balance elsewhere: You can do a trustee-to-trustee rollover to any custodian, once per year, tax-free. Consider consolidating to Fidelity for zero fees on a growing balance.

Pro tip: You can open an HSA at any custodian regardless of which insurance plan you have, as long as the plan is HSA-eligible. You're not locked in by your insurer.

Frequently asked questions

Can I switch HSA custodians?

Yes. You can do a trustee-to-trustee transfer once per 12-month period with no taxes or penalties. The process takes 2–4 weeks and your balance moves intact.

Do I need to open an HSA through my insurance company?

No. Your HSA account is completely separate from your health insurance plan. You can open one at any custodian as long as you're enrolled in an HSA-eligible plan.

What's the deadline to contribute for 2026?

You can contribute to your 2026 HSA until April 15, 2027 — the federal tax filing deadline. This means you can open an account late in the year and still max out your contribution for tax purposes.
