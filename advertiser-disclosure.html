How myHSAPath Makes Money

myHSAPath is an independent content website. We keep the site free to use through the following revenue sources:

1. Affiliate Referral Commissions

Our primary revenue source. When you click a link to an HSA custodian, insurance platform, or financial tool on our site and complete a qualifying action (typically opening an account), we may receive a referral fee from that company.

This fee is paid by the company from their marketing budget. It does not increase the cost of any product or account for you. Opening a Fidelity HSA through our link costs exactly the same as opening one directly through Fidelity.com.

Commissions typically range from $25 to $75 per referred account, depending on the custodian and their current affiliate program terms.

2. Newsletter Sponsorships

Our Beehiiv newsletter may include sponsored content from relevant financial companies. Sponsored newsletter content is clearly labeled as "Sponsored" or "Partner Content" at the top of the email section. Sponsors do not influence our editorial newsletter content.

3. Display Advertising

We may display programmatic advertisements through ad networks. These ads are served automatically based on page content and visitor behavior, not curated editorial relationships. Ad placements are visually distinct from editorial content.

4. Paid Tools (Future)

We may in future offer premium calculator tools or personalized HSA optimization reports for a one-time or subscription fee. Any paid features will be clearly labeled.

How We Rate HSA Accounts

Our ratings are based on a weighted scoring methodology applied consistently across all custodians. The factors and weights are:

35%
Fees & Costs
Monthly account fees, investment fees, transaction charges, and fee waiver conditions. Zero-fee custodians score maximum points here.
30%
Investment Options
Breadth of investment choices, minimum investment thresholds, fund expense ratios, and brokerage platform quality.
20%
User Experience
Mobile app quality, account opening speed, customer support responsiveness, and ease of use for self-employed users.
15%
Trust & Safety
FDIC/SIPC insurance coverage, institution history, financial stability, and HSA-specific experience and specialization.

Scores are assigned on a 10-point scale per factor. The final rating is a weighted average rounded to one decimal place. Scores are reviewed and updated when custodians materially change their products, fees, or features.

Data sources: Our ratings draw on publicly available information from each custodian's website, fee schedules, and product documentation, supplemented by direct account testing where conducted. We link to primary sources wherever possible.

Our Editorial Independence Policy

myHSAPath maintains a strict separation between our commercial relationships and our editorial content. This policy governs how we operate:

Who decides what we publish?

Editorial decisions — including which products to review, how to rate them, and what to recommend — are made solely by our editorial team based on our methodology and research. Affiliate partners and advertisers have no input into editorial decisions.

What happens if we earn more from one company?

Nothing changes editorially. If Lively pays us a higher commission than Fidelity, and Fidelity is the better product for most users, we still rank Fidelity first. Our ratings reflect product quality, not commission rates.

What if a company asks us to change a rating?

We decline. If a company believes a factual error exists in our coverage, we welcome that feedback and will investigate and correct genuine errors. We will not change ratings or rankings in response to commercial pressure.

What if a company stops their affiliate program?

We continue to cover them editorially. Companies without affiliate relationships are included in our comparisons if they serve our readers. Being outside our affiliate program does not affect whether we cover a product or how we rate it.

FTC Compliance Statement

myHSAPath complies with the Federal Trade Commission's guidelines concerning the use of endorsements and testimonials in advertising (16 CFR Part 255) and the FTC's Disclosures guidelines for online advertising.

Specifically:

  • We disclose material connections (affiliate relationships) clearly and conspicuously on every page that contains affiliate links
  • Our disclosure language appears near the top of comparison pages, not buried in footers
  • We use clear, plain-English disclosure language that a reasonable consumer can understand
  • We do not use deceptive formatting to obscure the commercial nature of affiliate links

How to Identify Affiliate Links on Our Site

We want you to always know when a link might earn us a commission. Here's how to spot them:

  • Comparison page CTAs: Buttons labeled "Open Account →" or "Open Free →" on our custodian comparison pages are affiliate links
  • In-content links: Links to custodian websites within blog posts and review articles are typically affiliate links
  • Sidebar recommendations: "Our Pick" and "Editor's Choice" links in article sidebars are affiliate links
  • Non-affiliate links: Links to IRS.gov, HealthCare.gov, and other government or educational resources are never affiliate links

If you ever want to verify whether a specific link is an affiliate link, you're welcome to email us at editorial@myhsapath.com and we'll tell you directly.
